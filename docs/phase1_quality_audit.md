## Baseline two-hour hyperglycemia

Positive = any of the next eight observed CGM readings strictly >180 mg/dL. No interpolation is used for labels. Windows with any future sensor failure are excluded; the final eight timestamps per patient have no complete horizon. Overlapping windows are not independent events.

The all-window target includes persistent hyperglycemia and is not exclusively postprandial. The post-meal subset is therefore reported separately. These are descriptive synthetic rates, not disease prevalence or model performance.

## Provenance and limits

These datasets are newly generated from an illustrative stochastic state-space simulator. They were not generated by running Synthea, are not fitted to MIMIC-IV biomarker distributions, and are not calibrated to CGMacros response kinetics. No real EHR, CGM, genetic or wearable records were accessed. India is a scenario label, not demographic representativeness.

Synthea, MIMIC-IV and CGMacros are design references only. Source pages were consulted to establish what these projects contain, not to estimate model parameters:

- Synthea: https://synthea.mitre.org/ and https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea
- MIMIC-IV: https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/
- CGMacros: https://physionet.org/content/cgmacros/

The schema is a project-specific normalized two-table contract, not an exact native Synthea or MIMIC schema. Apple Health, Google Fit and FreeStyle Libre integrations are not implemented or device-validated.

Synthetic patient hold-outs can still exploit shared simulator assumptions. They cannot establish real-world accuracy, fairness, clinical safety or causal intervention benefit. No non-overfitting guarantee or regulatory-compliance certification is made.

No model training or dashboard is performed in Phase 1. The 90:10 experiment would contain only the four deliberately chosen archetypes as evaluation patients; this is a stress test, not an unbiased population estimate. Phase 2 should separate patient-level calibration, freeze model choices without test-based selection, report patient-cluster uncertainty, and retain this limitation.

A forecast classifier operating on edited observed covariates does not by itself estimate a future treatment effect. Phase 3 must distinguish hypothetical scenarios from validated causal intervention effects.
